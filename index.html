Before you enquire.

Can I start without a passport?

Yes. You can discuss overseas work while seeking passport assistance. Tell us your current document status when you enquire.

Can I get help only with documents?

Yes. Passport, PCC, verification and visa documentation support can be discussed separately from recruitment.

Is a job or visa guaranteed?

No. Jobs depend on employer selection and available roles; visa decisions rest with the relevant authorities. Ask for the requirements, terms and charges before proceeding.
